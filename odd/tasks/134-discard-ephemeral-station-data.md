## Objective
Explicit, idempotent discard of an active or completed station through the public context and Phoenix API; explicit restart/result leave waits for acknowledgement, and abrupt page exit makes one best-effort keepalive attempt. The existing 30-minute ETS TTL remains a fallback.

## Constraints and decisions
- `BackendWeb` calls only `Backend.Stations`; no database, raw audio, retained learner history, or transcript logging.
- Never confuse station completion with discard; result reads must not extend the station deadline or safety TTL.
- Explicit discard errors must not clear local state or start another session without acknowledgement; allow retry. Abrupt page exit cannot await delivery and does not guarantee it.
