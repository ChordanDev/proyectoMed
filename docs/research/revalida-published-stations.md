# Published Revalida practical-exam stations (INEP)

Compiled on 2026-10-29 for the station simulator backlog. Question: which practical-stage (segunda etapa, prova de habilidades clínicas) stations has INEP published, edition by edition, so the simulator can cover every case type? Feeds issue #322 (multi-actor stations).

## Method and source policy

- Start point: the INEP page `Provas e Gabaritos` of the Revalida (gov.br/inep, one tab per year, listing 2011-2017 and 2020-2026). Each year tab was read directly and every link to the practical stage (`Prova de habilidades clínicas` and `Padrão Esperado de Procedimentos`, PEP) was followed to `download.inep.gov.br`.
- Every station below comes from those INEP PDFs only: the `Instruções ao participante` (cenário de atuação, descrição do caso, tarefas, impressos) and the PEP (síntese da estação or checklist). Secondary sources (prep-course sites) were used only to find dates and to cross-check that INEP news existed; they are not cited for any station.
- Text PDFs were converted with `pdftotext`. The 2020 PEP and the 2020 reapplication papers are scanned images and were read with on-device OCR, so their details are less certain.
- This file stores metadata only (edition, number, area, setting, short theme, actors, task types, URL). No station script, checklist, patient answers or scoring items are reproduced; themes are paraphrased in a few words. The `Prova` + `PEP` pair of the edition is the source of each row (reference links at the end).
- Legend for the `Actors` column: `Patient` = adult simulated patient, only talking actor; `Mother/Father/Wife informant` = the companion answers and the patient is silent or simulated; `Adolescent alone`; `Nurse reports` = third-party informant; `mannequin` = part of the task is done on a model, doll or device; `examiner` = findings are verbalised to the evaluator (`chefe de estação`).

The 2022/1 reapplication re-publishes the 2022/2 set and adds none. Every regular edition has 10 stations: 2 per area, ordered Clínica Médica, Cirurgia, Pediatria, Ginecologia e Obstetrícia, MFC/Saúde Coletiva (stations 1-5), then the same sequence again (stations 6-10). Each station lasts 10 minutes.

### 3.3 Recurring themes (case types the simulator should model)

- Infectious and notifiable: dengue (2021 grp B, 2024/2 grp A, 2025/1 in pregnancy), tuberculosis follow-up, pertussis, cutaneous leishmaniasis, herpes zoster, hepatitis serologies, syphilis, PID/STI with partner management, caterpillar and scorpion envenomation.
- Chronic disease follow-up in APS: hypertension (2023/1, 2025/2), diabetes and insulin start (2024/1), prediabetes (2025/2), obesity (2023/2), smoking cessation, insomnia, burnout, osteoporosis.
- Emergency / surgical: abdominal pain (pancreatitis, cholecystitis, perforated ulcer, obstruction, renal colic, hernia), trauma and burns (the same ATLS trauma case appears in 2021 and 2024/1 and the burns case in 2022/1 and 2024/1), stroke (2023/2, 2024/2), limb ischaemia, splenic rupture, testicular torsion.
- Women's health: contraception, Pap smear and breast exam (hands-on technique), prenatal exam on mannequin, gestational diabetes, HELLP, placenta praevia, abruption, abortion/unplanned pregnancy, sexual violence, climacteric, fibroids, PCOS/amenorrhoea, puerperium.
- Paediatrics: well-child visits and growth charts, vaccine reactions and schedule, neonatal fever and jaundice, febrile seizure, appendicitis, pyloric stenosis, cellulitis, intussusception, foreign body, anaphylaxis, sickle cell crisis, DKA, cryptorchidism/phimosis, child hypertension, ASD screening.
- Communication-heavy tasks: breaking bad news (2024/1 st. 5), discrimination and LGBT care (2023/2), violence screening and notification (2020 st. 10, 2024/2 st. 9), orphaned infant and father (2023/1), community diagnosis with an indigenous health agent (2023/1 st. 5).
- Procedures and hands-on tasks (mannequin, doll or device) appear in at least 11 cases: abdominal palpation and ascites manoeuvres, gynaecological exam, Pap collection, breast exam, obstetric exam, suturing, anthropometry on a doll, insulin preparation, inhaler technique, needle decompression, NIHSS.
- Documents and notification: prescriptions or certificates are requested explicitly in only a few stations (2025/2 st. 4 and 6; work certificate in 2020 st. 5; notification in 2020 st. 10, 2022/1 st. 5 and 2024/2 st. 5).

## 4. Gaps and unverified items

- Editions 2011-2017: INEP's tabs list only the objective and discursive papers and their keys; no practical-stage (habilidades clínicas) station is published for them. Marked as a gap; nothing was taken from secondary sources.
- No INEP tab exists for 2018 or 2019 (no edition listed).
- Revalida 2026/1 and 2026/2: the 2026 tab lists only the written papers and keys. As of 2026-10-29 no practical-stage document was found. Secondary reports mention a proposed second-stage date for 2026/1 and for 2026/2, neither confirmed by an INEP document read here.
- Revalida 2025/2: the INEP page links the preliminary PEP only (`revalida_2025_2_pep_premilminar.pdf`). Secondary news says a definitive PEP followed in July 2026; it was not found on the INEP page, so station details may have been corrected after appeals.
- Practical-exam dates come from INEP news headlines surfaced through search; the news pages could not be opened (restricted notice), so dates were not confirmed in the page body. The year for Revalida 2020 (10-11 Jul, shown here as 2021) comes from a search summary and should be double-checked. Reapplication dates for 2020 and 2021 were not found.
- 2020 PEP, 2020 reapplication paper and PEP are scanned images; themes there rely on OCR and on headings. 2020 st. 9 (puérpera) diagnosis is inferred from the checklist item types, and 2020 st. 7 is reported as the two differentials the PEP asks to explain, not a single diagnosis.
- 2021 reapplication st. R5 (routine consult, 53 y woman): the main diagnosis or theme was not extracted from the PEP. The 2021 reapplication pairs station slots (1 and 6, 2 and 7, ...), so it is counted as 5 cases.
- Stations of 2022/1 reapplication are the 2022/2 stations; they are not counted twice. The 2022/2 PEP carries a correction (item 12 of station 2 annulled).
- Themes are summarised from the síntese or the checklist headings, not from a full reading of every checklist. Where a station has several teaching goals (for example 2023/1 st. 2, 2024/2 st. 10), only the main theme is listed.
- Actor tags come from the instructions and síntese: for some stations the paper does not say whether a companion is present, so 'Patient' was recorded. Stations marked with 'mannequin' were counted from explicit mentions of a mannequin, doll or device; others may involve hands-on steps that are only verbalised.
- 2023/2 st. 4 (breast exam): the paper requires the full exam sequence but also says the patient is not touched; the model or verbalisation used was not confirmed.
- Copyright/originality: the PDFs are INEP publications; this file keeps metadata and paraphrases only. Any station script for the simulator must be authored from clinical guidelines, not copied from INEP.
